## Domain Decomposition

Topology is provisionally framed as a formal field where equivalence, invariance, and construction determine the learning order. This scaffold records assumptions for review; it is not a finished report.

## Orientation

Use this scaffold to plan source review and curriculum structure before writing a human-facing report.
